One line for each thing GM-Apprentice does, grouped the way a campaign
runs. Each one links to a tutorial where there is one, and to the
plugin's own guide where there isn't. You never need the skill names in
the right-hand column: say what you want in plain words and the right
one picks it up.

Five game systems have rules built in: Call of Cthulhu 7th edition
(Regency Cthulhu too), GURPS 4th edition, Forged in the Dark (Blades in
the Dark), D&D 5e (2024 rules) and Pathfinder 2e (Remaster). The rules
come from the free, open rules texts (SRD and ORC), and for GURPS from
the plugin's own summaries. Anything else from a book you own
can go in [your own rulebook files](/tutorials/your-own-books/).

## Organize

| What it does | Skill |
|---|---|
| Keeps your campaign as a folder of linked notes: one for every character, place, faction, item and session. Obsidian opens it, but you don't need Obsidian. [Organize guide](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/campaign-organizer.md) | `campaign-organizer` |
| Brings in a campaign you've already been running: notes, character sheets, maps, transcripts, spreadsheets. It asks you what really happened at the table and never deletes your files. [Tutorial](/tutorials/bring-in-a-campaign/) | `vault-ingest` |
| Writes the links between notes, with who is tied to whom and how they feel about it, so Obsidian's graph can draw your campaign. | `campaign-organizer` |
| Keeps a timeline: each wrap-up adds what happened and when. | `session-wrapup` |
| Checks your campaign for mistakes: contradictions, dead NPCs who turn up again, names that are too alike, clues with no backup, spoilers left lying in the open. You decide on each one; nothing changes without your yes. [Check guide](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/campaign-qa.md) | `campaign-qa` |
| Keeps your house rules in one place and puts them ahead of the rulebook at the table. [Tutorial](/tutorials/house-rules/) | `session-play` |
| Updates an older campaign folder when GM-Apprentice changes, after taking a backup. You don't run anything. | `campaign-organizer` |

## Prep

| What it does | Skill |
|---|---|
| Builds a new campaign, one-shot or chapter with you, from a half-idea or from nothing, and gets session one ready to prep. [Tutorial](/tutorials/grow-a-campaign/) | `the-midwife` |
| Preps your next session: what happened last time, what got skipped, each character's moment, open threads, and scenes to choose from. [Prep guide](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/session-prep.md) | `session-prep` |
| Answers rules questions, citing the file it found the answer in. | `ttrpg-expert` |
| Makes characters to the rules, checking the numbers as it goes: a new player's character, an NPC with stats, a monster. [Tutorial](/tutorials/character-sheets/) | `ttrpg-expert` |
| Writes NPCs, scenes, encounters, places, factions, items and handouts (letters, newspapers, telegrams). New material stays a draft until you accept it. [Rules and content guide](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/ttrpg-expert.md) | `ttrpg-expert` |
| Checks for plot holes and for players who haven't had a moment lately. | `ttrpg-expert` |
| Uses the books you own, kept on your computer and never shared. [Tutorial](/tutorials/your-own-books/) | `ttrpg-expert` |

## Run

| What it does | Skill |
|---|---|
| Helps at the table: who's this NPC, what did they say last time, what's the rule, give me a shopkeeper now. Short answers, and nothing is saved until you say so. [At-the-table guide](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/session-play.md) | `session-play` |
| Gives you a GM copy of your site with every secret showing, on your tablet or phone over your home network. It never goes online. [Tutorial](/tutorials/gm-site/) | `publish-site` |
| Lets players change their own hit points and conditions on their sheets during play, with a party board that keeps up. For Call of Cthulhu, GURPS and D&D, on a Cloudflare site. [Tutorial](/tutorials/players-at-the-table/) | `publish-site` |
| Gives each player a box on their character page for requests and questions. The apprentice makes the clean sheet changes for you and holds the rest for you to decide. Cloudflare sites only. [Tutorial](/tutorials/players-at-the-table/) | `publish-site` |
| Works out a GURPS character's chance to hit, on their sheet. | `publish-site` |

## Publish

| What it does | Skill |
|---|---|
| Turns your rough notes into a written recap, and updates every character, place and thread the session touched. [Wrap-up guide](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/session-wrapup.md) | `session-wrapup` |
| Publishes a website for your players, free on Cloudflare or GitHub: the story so far, a page for everyone and everything they've met, search, and a timeline. [Tutorial](/tutorials/publish-your-site/) | `publish-site` |
| Keeps secrets off the site until play reveals them, and checks for leaks before it publishes. | `publish-site` |
| Shows full character sheets for Call of Cthulhu, GURPS and D&D, with a skin and portrait frame to suit. Pathfinder and Forged in the Dark get simpler sheets for now. [Tutorial](/tutorials/character-sheets/) | `publish-site` |
| Keeps a D&D character in step with its D&D Beyond page. [Tutorial](/tutorials/character-sheets/) | `publish-site` |
| Shows the party on one page: who's in play, how they're doing, and what's still open. | `publish-site` |
| Changes the site's look: five genre styles, your own colours and fonts, light and dark, a banner for the front page. [Tutorial](/tutorials/change-the-look/) | `publish-site` |
| Draws portraits and pictures of places and scenes, checks each one and redraws the bad ones. No extra cost on a ChatGPT plan through Codex, or paid on your own Google key within limits you set. [Tutorial](/tutorials/add-pictures/) | `campaign-organizer` |
| Puts a small preview card on every link, and a Connections section on each page showing who is tied to whom. | `publish-site` |
