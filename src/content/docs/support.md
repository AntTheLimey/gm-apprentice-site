GM-Apprentice is free and made by one GM, so help comes from me and from
other GMs who use it. Pick whichever of these fits.

## Something went wrong

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/issues/new).
Say what you asked for, what happened instead, and which game system you
play. Your apprentice can write the report for you. It searches for an
existing report first, shows you its draft, and files it only when you
say yes.

## A question, or an idea

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/discussions),
where other GMs can answer too and the answer helps the next person.

## Tell me how it went

I like hearing how it went at your table, good or bad.
[Tell us how it went](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/discussions/new?category=show-and-tell).

## Email

For anything else, or anything you'd rather not post in public, write to
**support@gmapprentice.com**.

To report a security problem, email it there too. Please don't post it in
a public issue.
