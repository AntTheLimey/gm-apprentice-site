Every campaign here was published from a GM's vault: the session recaps,
character sheets, maps and timelines are what the vault already held, built
into a site for the players by GM-Apprentice (or, for The Long Lease, by
GM-Scriptorium, which reads the same vaults). The GM's secrets stay in the
vault.
