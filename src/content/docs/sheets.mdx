Every player character is one note in your vault. The campaign site turns
that note into a full sheet for Call of Cthulhu, GURPS or D&D, dressed in
the campaign's own look. These are three real sheets from three real
tables. Click through the tabs: most of the good stuff is below the top
of the sheet.

## At the Table

Players keep their own sheet open on their phone. A hit lands, they tap
minus, and the party roster on your screen changes. Each sheet also has a
box for requests and questions: "spend 1 xp to raise Streetwise". Your
apprentice makes the clean changes for you and holds anything that needs
your ruling.

<div class="shot-pair not-content">
<Shot src="/img/sh-phone-hp.jpg" alt="Six's sheet on a phone: portrait, then hit points 13 of 13 and fatigue points 12 of 12 with minus and plus buttons" narrow>
Hit points and fatigue points, one tap each.
</Shot>
<Shot src="/img/sh-phone-ask.jpg" alt="The request box on a phone: type a change or a question, with a Send button and the request history" narrow>

Both work for Call of Cthulhu, GURPS and D&D on a site hosted on
Cloudflare Pages, and are off until you ask for them. See
[Players at the table](/tutorials/players-at-the-table/).

## Making Your Own

- [Character sheets](/tutorials/character-sheets/) builds a character to
  the rules and shows the sheet on your site. D&D characters can stay in
  step with D&D Beyond. Pathfinder and Forged in the Dark characters get a
  simpler page for now.
- [Change the look](/tutorials/change-the-look/) gives the sheets a skin,
  such as parchment or a case file, and a portrait frame.
- The [Gallery](/gallery/) has the whole campaign sites these sheets
  come from.
