title: Start from your notes
description: Turn a pile of old campaign notes into a vault you can play from, with vault-ingest.

---

You've run a game already. The notes are in text files, documents, a
notebook you photographed, a character sheet or two. In one conversation
GM-Apprentice sorts the pile, asks you what really happened at the table,
and builds a vault: a page per session with a recap, a note for every
person, place and item, the threads still open, and your undecided
mysteries kept undecided. Your original files are left alone.

Everything below is from a real run on GM-Apprentice 1.12.98. We typed the
GM's side for this page; the replies are the agent's own, trimmed (`[...]`)
but not reworded.

New to all this? [Get set up](/start/setup/) first.

## Try it on our pile first

**[Download the notes pile](/downloads/notes-pile.zip)** (a made-up
two-session D&D one-shot, about 650 words): two sessions' scrawled
notes, an NPC list, one character sheet, a file of loose ideas and a
hand-drawn village map. A name is spelled two ways, the ideas file mixes
in things that never happened, and nobody wrote down the year. Like real
notes.

Or use your own. Put everything in one folder.

## 1. Open your agent next to the notes and ask

> [!you]
>
> I've got a folder of old notes from a one-shot I ran, in notes-pile. Can
> you turn them into a vault so I can pick the game back up?

It reads everything first and writes nothing yet:

> [!apprentice]
>
> | File | Type | What it covers | When |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | `session 1 notes.txt` | Notes from play | Hollin Ford, Tobin missing, Pell lifts the reeve's letter, the cellar rat fight, a trapdoor locked from below | Session 1, 14 Mar |
> | `loose ideas.txt` | Brainstorm (didn't happen at the table) | Ringing the bell floods the pool, Corwin's debt, the clapper fits the bell, Maude goes missing in session 3, the church bell has no clapper | Ideas for session 3 |
>
> [...] Your open questions stay open: I'll mark whether the Bell Man is
> Aldric as undecided and keep who "R." is as a loose thread. The loose
> ideas go into the session 3 planning notes, not into what happened.

Then it asks the few things it can't work out: where the vault goes, which
rules edition, what year the sessions were, and a name for the campaign.

## 2. Answer, and say yes to the preview

> [!you]
>
> A new Hollin Ford folder next to notes-pile is good. 2024 rules. The
> sessions were March 2026. Call it The Bell Man of Hollin Ford.

It shows what it will create before it creates it. Say yes.

## 3. The interview

This is the part that matters. Notes say what happened in a hurry; the
interview recovers what you meant. It asks about each player character,
then about the gaps, one question at a time:

> [!apprentice]
>
> **The Bell Man:** In the chamber, Hale tanked it and Oswin cast Cure
> Wounds twice. It said "Rendal" before it "went down." Was it destroyed
> for good, or did it just fall back into the water, so it could still be
> down there?

> [!you]
>
> It sank back into the water. The players think it's gone, but it's still
> down there.

> [!apprentice]
>
> Recorded. In the vault, the Bell Man is **still active** at the bottom of
> the chamber pool. The players believe it's gone, so that fact will go in
> GM-only sections that never appear on a player-facing page.

"Haven't decided, leave it open" is a fine answer. It stays open.

## 4. Settle the last few things

At the end it lists the small calls left: which threads to close, a
character's old injury, missing stat sheets, and a suggested voice for
future recaps. Answer in one line.

## What you have now

```text
Hollin Ford/
  Chapters/Chapter 1/Sessions/Session 01/   play notes (yours, word for word), a page, a wrap-up
  Chapters/Chapter 1/Sessions/Session 02/
  Chapters/Chapter 1/Planning/Session 3 Ideas.md   your loose ideas, marked not canon
  Characters/PCs/        Pell Underhill, Oswin, Bryony, Hale, each with a story page
  Characters/NPCs/       Maude Tanner, Corwin Rendal, Aldric Rendal, Brother Eamon, Tobin, Hobb
  Creatures/The Bell Man.md
  Locations/             Hollin Ford (with the map), the mill, the chamber, the Goose, the church
  Items & Artifacts/     the silver bell clapper, Tobin's whistle
  Documents/The Reeve's Letter.md
```

Each session's wrap-up has a recap you can read to your players. From
"Pell picked it, took ages, nat 1 first try lol":

> [!vault]
>
> [[Pell Underhill]] set to work on the trapdoor locked from below. His first
> try went badly wrong, and the lock took ages, but it opened.

and GM-only notes for picking the game back up: who carries what, what
each player character wants, and the threads still open.
