A campaign goes round the same loop: **organize** what you know,
**prep** the next session, **run** it, then **publish** what happened.
Each tutorial below does one job in that loop, from a real run on the
[Snowbound sample vault](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice-snowbound/tree/sample-v2).
The skill behind each one is named in brackets. You never have to call a
skill by name: say what you want and the right one picks it up.

New here? [Start here](/start/setup/) first: it gets you set up and
ends with a vault of your own.

## Organize

- [Grow a campaign from an idea](/tutorials/grow-a-campaign/): a spark,
  or nothing at all, turned into a campaign you can prep (the-midwife).
- [Bring in an existing campaign](/tutorials/bring-in-a-campaign/): old
  notes, sheets and transcripts turned into a vault (vault-ingest).
- [Organize your notes](/tutorials/organize-your-notes/): new NPCs and
  places filed as linked notes, checked against canon (campaign-organizer).
- [House rules](/tutorials/house-rules/): where your table's rules live,
  and how they beat the book.
- [Check your vault](/tutorials/check-your-vault/): contradictions,
  broken links and loose ends found before the players find them
  (campaign-qa).

## Prep

- [Prep a session](/tutorials/prep-a-session/): a plan built from what
  happened last time (session-prep).
- [Rules, NPCs and ideas](/tutorials/rules-npcs-and-ideas/): rules
  answers, stat blocks and new material on request (ttrpg-expert).
- [Your own rulebooks](/tutorials/your-own-books/): let the apprentice
  use books you own.

## Run

- [At the table](/tutorials/at-the-table/): fast answers and notes
  taken while you play (session-play).
- [Players at the table](/tutorials/players-at-the-table/): players mark
  their own sheets, and their requests reach you (publish-site).

## Publish

- [Wrap up a session](/tutorials/wrap-up-a-session/): play notes turned
  into a recap, updated notes and what carries forward (session-wrapup).
- [Set up Cloudflare](/tutorials/set-up-cloudflare/): a free account and
  a token, once, so your apprentice can publish there.
- [Publish your site](/tutorials/publish-your-site/): a campaign website
  for your players, with your secrets left out (publish-site).
- [The GM site](/tutorials/gm-site/): a private copy with every secret
  showing, for your tablet (publish-site).

## Customizing

- [Connections](/tutorials/connections/): who and what each page is
  tied to, drawn as a tree, a web or cards (publish-site).
- [Handouts](/tutorials/handouts/): letters, clippings and printouts,
  laid out to print and shown when the players find them (ttrpg-expert,
  publish-site).
- [Character sheets](/tutorials/character-sheets/): full sheets on the
  site, built to the rules (publish-site).
- [Add pictures](/tutorials/add-pictures/): your own art, or pictures
  drawn for what's missing.
- [Change the look](/tutorials/change-the-look/): themes, colours,
  lettering and sheet skins (publish-site).

## Without an agent

- [Do it by hand](/tutorials/do-it-by-hand/): a vault, its checks and
  the players' website, made with GM-Apprentice's own commands and no
  agent at all.
