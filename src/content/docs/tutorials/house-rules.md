Every table has them. GM-Apprentice keeps yours in one place, the
**House Rules** section of your Campaign Overview
(`_Campaign/Campaign Overview.md`), written the way you'd say them at the
table. From there:

- **At the table**, a rules question checks your house rules before the
  book.
- **In prep**, the session plan carries them, so they're in front of you.
- **On the campaign site**, they're always public: players get a House
  Rules page linked from the home page, and the rules show on the
  Campaign Overview page too.

## Three ways in

1. **When the campaign starts.** The Midwife asks for them as its last
   setup question (see [Grow a campaign from an idea](/tutorials/grow-a-campaign/)).
2. **In the middle of a session.** Just say it.
3. **By hand.** Open the Campaign Overview and add a bullet under
   `## House Rules`. It's an ordinary Markdown list.

## A rule made mid-session, from a real run

On GM-Apprentice 1.12.98, using the [Snowbound sample vault](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice-snowbound/tree/sample-v2)
(clone or download tag `sample-v2` to follow along). We typed the GM's
side; the replies are the agent's own, trimmed (`[...]`) but not reworded.

> We're playing session 5 right now. New house rule from tonight: a natural
> 1 on a death save counts as one failure, not two.

> I've added the rule. It's under a new **House Rules** section in
> `_Campaign/Campaign Overview.md`: *Death saves: a natural 1 counts as one
> failure, not two (from Session 5).*

Later that night, it matters:

> Nim is at 0 HP with one failed death save already, and she's just rolled a
> natural 1. What happens?

> Nim now has **2 failed death saves**. Under tonight's house rule the
> natural 1 counts as one failure, not two, so she isn't dead yet.
>
> She's still dying, and one more failure kills her. [...]

By the book, that roll would have killed her. After the session, a local
build of the site has the rule on its own page:

![The Snowbound campaign site's House Rules page, listing the death save rule added in session 5](/img/tutorials/house-rules-page.jpg)

## Good to know

- **Write them as you'd say them.** "Flanking: two allies on opposite
  sides of a foe both get advantage" beats legal language.
  The apprentice reads them as written.
- **Note when a rule started.** "(from Session 5)" tells you, and your
  players, why an old recap played it differently.
- **Your own rulebooks are a different thing.** House rules are a few
  lines. If you want the apprentice to know a whole supplement, see
  [personal reference files](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice/blob/main/docs/personal-reference-files.md)
  in the plugin docs.
