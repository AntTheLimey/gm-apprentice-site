The skill behind this is **session-play**. It's built for speed: short
questions, short answers, only what you need right now. Keep the
conversation open beside you while you run the game and type to it as you
would mutter to a co-GM.

## When to reach for it

- You've forgotten who someone is, or what happened to an item.
- A rules question comes up and you don't want to stop for the book.
- The players walk into somewhere you didn't plan, and you need a name, a
  shopkeeper, or what's on the desk.
- Something happens that you'll want to remember after the session.

Starting phrases that work:

- "Remind me who Bram is."
- "Can she grapple while prone?"
- "I need a bartender for this scene."
- "Note: they went to the docks instead of the library."

## From a real run

On GM-Apprentice 1.12.98, using the [Snowbound sample vault](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice-snowbound/tree/sample-v2)
(clone or download tag `sample-v2` to follow along), with the plan from
[Prep a session](/tutorials/prep-a-session/) in place. We typed the GM's
side; the replies are the agent's own, trimmed (`[...]`) but not reworded.

> [!you]
>
> We're starting session 5. The party's just reached the mule-yard. Remind
> me who Bram is.

> [!apprentice]
>
> **Bram** runs the mule-yard and is Edda Marsh's cousin. He sold Tam Rowe
> his mule on credit last summer. In the autumn the mule came back down the
> pass alone, and he's fed it all winter.
>
> **Scene 1 opener (your Session 5 plan):** Bram is at the yard gate with a
> halter in his hand. He sees the shape on the hurdle and knows the coat.
> [...]
>
> Stats are a stock Commoner (AC 10, HP 4, club +2 for 1d4).

Later, a rules question:

> [!you]
>
> Now they're at the Stag & Lantern. Nim wants to slip out the back before
> the collector sees her. What does she roll?

> [!apprentice]
>
> **Dexterity (Stealth) at +10.** She has Expertise in it, and the bonus
> stays the same at level 6. Set the DC with the collector's passive
> Perception. He has no stat block, so pick one: about 12 for a plain
> clerk, or 15–16 if he's a pro. The plan says he "expects it", which
> points to the higher number. [...]
>
> If she fails, your contingency triggers ("run from the collector and it
> fails"). His two Toughs come in from the inn yard and the collector stays
> in his seat.

The collector was never given a name in prep:

> [!you]
>
> He needs a name, quick.

> [!apprentice]
>
> **Mattias Pell.** He's soft-spoken and wears clean gloves, and he calls
> Nim "Miss Underbough" every time.
>
> If you'd rather another: **Corwin Thresh** or **Jory Lisk**.
>
> [...] Want me to save the collector to the vault?

And the roll goes badly:

> [!you]
>
> Note: Nim rolled a 4. The collector caught her at the back door, and
> Rosie paid him off with her own gold leaf.

> [!apprentice]
>
> I've saved that to the new Session 5 Play Notes file. [...]
>
> Since the debt is now closed, the collector won't be waiting at the
> burying gate in Scene 4.
>
> I wrote down Mattias Pell as the name I suggested, not one you've
> confirmed. If you used a different name at the table, tell me and I'll
> correct it.

The play notes it keeps are rough on purpose, tagged for the wrap-up to
pick up later:

> [!vault]
>
> Scene 3: [[Nim Underbough]] tried to slip out the back before the
> collector saw her. Stealth roll = 4, fail. Collector caught her at the
> back door.
>
> NEW-EVENT: [[Rosie Tallowby]] paid the collector off with her own gold
> leaf. Nim's debt settled.
>
> NEW-NPC: the creditor's collector. Name suggested at the table: Mattias
> Pell (alternates Corwin Thresh, Jory Lisk; GM hasn't confirmed which).
> [...] Not saved to vault.

## How it behaves

- **Short.** One to five sentences, no lectures. Brief in, brief out.
- **Your plan first.** It reads the session plan, so answers come with
  what you prepared: the scene, the NPC's wants, your contingencies.
- **Your house rules before the book.** See [House rules](/tutorials/house-rules/).
- **Nothing invented becomes canon on its own.** A name or NPC made up on
  the spot stays provisional until you say keep it. Unsaved ones are
  flagged for the wrap-up.
- **Notes can be scraps.** "Note:" and a fragment is enough. The wrap-up
  turns them into the record.
- **Picks up mid-scene.** If last session stopped part-way through a
  scene, play opens where it stood.

## Other things to ask it

- "Where did they leave the ritual dagger?"
- "Give me three options for where the cult could be meeting."
- "The players broke into an office. What's on the desk?"
- "Mark that Inspector Barrow is now suspicious of them."

## What comes next

When the session ends, [wrap it up](/tutorials/wrap-up-a-session/): hand
over your own notes and the apprentice turns them, with its play notes,
into the record.
